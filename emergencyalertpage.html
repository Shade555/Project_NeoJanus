BE READY FOR ANY DISASTER

EMERGENCY QUICK GUIDE

Avalanche

Sudden flow of snow, ice, and rocks down a mountainside,
buries everything in its path within seconds.

Earthquake

Sudden shaking of the ground caused by tectonic movements,
can collapse buildings instantly.

Flood

Overflow of water onto normally dry land,
often leads to drowning and property damage.

Wildfire

Uncontrolled fire in forests or grasslands,
spreads rapidly and destroys homes and wildlife.

Landslide

Sudden downhill movement of rock, earth, or debris,
often triggered by heavy rain or earthquakes.

Tornado

Violently rotating air column extending from a storm to the ground,
extremely fast and destructive.

Tsunami

Giant sea waves triggered by underwater earthquakes,
can sweep away coastal towns in minutes.

Volcano

Explosion of magma, ash, and gases from a volcano,
can devastate areas within miles.
